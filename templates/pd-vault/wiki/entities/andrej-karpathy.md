# Andrej Karpathy

**Summary**: AI researcher and educator — co-founder of OpenAI and former Tesla AI director — and the originator of the "LLM wiki" pattern of keeping personal knowledge as LLM-readable markdown.

## Who

Co-founder of OpenAI and former director of AI at Tesla, widely known as one of the clearest explainers of machine-learning concepts. (raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md)

## Relevance here

Advocated the [[llm-wiki]] idea: store personal notes and documents in structured plain markdown so a coding agent / LLM can query, synthesize, and help maintain them — organizing information for a model to read rather than for human navigation. The pattern underpins this whole Second Brain vault.
